# Comparing Maintenance Policies for Wind Turbine Gearboxes Under Stochastic Degradation

Wind turbine gearboxes degrade under uncertain operating conditions, such as turbulence, storms, and rapidly chang- ing loads, making maintenance decisions sensitive to random- ness. While keeping turbines operating is crucial for energy production, poorly chosen maintenance strategies can lead to inadequate maintenance (higher failure risk and reduced out- put) or excessive maintenance (unnecessary cost and down- time). Inspired by previous work on this topic by Su et al., this paper uses a stochastic degradation model for the wind turbine gearbox’s health to compare the e"cacy of three maintenance approaches: condition-based maintenance, time- based incomplete maintenance, and a joint policy that com- bines the two. Deterministic analysis clarifies how each pol- icy shapes the health trajectory, and stochastic simulations (Euler-Maruyama) show how noise changes threshold cross- ings and maintenance timing across parameter choices.
